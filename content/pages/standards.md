These standards apply to everything Democracy Today publishes.

## Accuracy and sourcing

- Every news story carries a byline and a publication date. Updates are dated and time-stamped.
- Factual claims are verified against primary sources wherever possible: official records, government data, court documents, published research and on-the-record interviews. Those sources are linked in the story and listed at the end of it.
- When a claim cannot be confirmed, we say so explicitly or leave it out. We do not present unverified information as fact.
- We do not publish fabricated quotes, composite characters or invented examples presented as real.

## News and opinion

News reporting and opinion are separate. Editorials, columns, guest essays and other pieces that argue a point of view are labeled as opinion. Essays in our Ideas and Essays sections are signed and reflect the views of their authors.

## Elections

Democracy Today is not an election authority. For registration, deadlines, polling places and certified results, we direct readers to [vote.gov](https://vote.gov) and their state election office. On election night we report results only as they are released by official sources or called by a news organization we identify by name, with the time of each update. Results reported on election night are unofficial until certified by the states.

## Images

Photographs and illustrations are credited. Stock or illustrative images are labeled as such and are not presented as depicting the people or events in a story.

## Corrections

We correct errors promptly and transparently. See our [Corrections policy](/corrections/).

## Independence

Democracy Today is published by Atlas Media Network, LLC. We disclose our ownership on every page and are not affiliated with any other organization with a similar name.
