Democracy Today is a digital news and ideas publication covering U.S. politics and government, elections, Latino communities and the world, alongside essays, conversations and books about the ideas that sustain a free and open society. Our tagline, *Ideas for a more open tomorrow*, describes what we hope to contribute: reporting readers can verify and arguments they can engage with.

## Ownership

Democracy Today is a business unit of **Atlas Media Network, LLC**, 36 Russ Street, 3rd Floor, Hartford, CT 06106, United States. Atlas Media Network is the publisher and owner of Democracy Today. More information about the network is available at [atlasmedianetwork.org](https://www.atlasmedianetwork.org).

## Not affiliated with other organizations

Democracy Today is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any other news organization, publication, broadcast or program with a similar name.

## What we publish

- **News** is reported, signed and dated, and links to its primary sources. See our [Standards & Ethics](/standards/).
- **Opinion** — editorials, columns and essays — is clearly labeled and kept separate from news.
- **Election information** always points readers to official sources: [vote.gov](https://vote.gov) and state election offices.
