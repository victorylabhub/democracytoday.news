---

When we make a factual error, we correct it as soon as we confirm it. The correction is noted at the end of the story with the date and time it was made and a description of what changed. We do not silently alter published facts.

Minor changes that do not affect the meaning of a story, such as fixing a typo, may be made without a note.

## Report an error

If you believe we have published something inaccurate, write to [info@atlasmedianetwork.org](mailto:info@atlasmedianetwork.org?subject=Correction%20request) with the link to the story and the source that shows the correct information.
